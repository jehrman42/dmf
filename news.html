*** News ***

Delivery and installation of the new CFI/NBIF funded Hitachi SU3500 Variable Pressure SEM and Oxford AZtec Energy EDS sytem occurred in November and is nearing completion. Research projects are already underway with the new instrumentation. An Open House planned for 2016 to showcase the new features and capabilities of the equipment.

DMF generated SEM image of a diatom spore appears on the cover of the 2015 book Balastne Vode (Ballast Water) by Ante Kutle and Vladivoj Valkovic (in Croatian).

DMF generated SEM image of various diatoms demonstrating their size variation appears on the cover of the virtual issue of Journal of Plankton Research, organized by Associate Editors Beatrix Beisner and Zoe Finkel. This collection of papers focusing on plankton functional traits and functional group modelling, is freely available.

DMF generated SEM image of the diatom Thalassiosira licea appears on the cover of the November/December issue of Journal of Plankton Research, highlighting the research article "The diversity of diatom assemblages in ships' ballast sediments; colonization and propagule pressure on Canadian ports" by MC Villac, I Kaczmarska and JM Ehrman (available online).

Yet again, the DMF generated SEM image of a human white blood has been used for education/entertainment. This time the image appears in the iOS app "Cosmic Eye". A demonstration of the app is available on YouTube. The colorized version of this image has been used again by NIH/NIGMS, appearing in the article "Cell Migration: Right and Wrong Moves", available at both the NIGMS website and on LiveScience.com.

DMF SEM images of a variety of diatom sizes and shapes (right) and inside and outside views of Triceratium were recently chosen as cover art for the new edition of The Diatoms: Applications for the Environmental and Earth Sciences edited by E.F. Stoermer and J.P. Smol (Cambridge University Press).
